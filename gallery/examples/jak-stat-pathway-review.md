# JAK-STAT Pathway Review

## Use Case

Pathway diagram for an immunology review summarizing cytokine receptor activation, JAK phosphorylation, STAT dimerization, nuclear translocation, and therapeutic inhibition.

## Layout Plan

- Canvas: landscape 16:9
- Reading direction: extracellular space to nucleus
- Zones: extracellular ligand, membrane receptor, cytoplasm, nucleus
- Main visual hierarchy: canonical pathway plus inhibitor callouts

## Ready-to-Use Prompt

```text
Create a publication-ready biomedical schematic, BioRender-inspired but fully original.

Topic: Canonical JAK-STAT cytokine signaling and pharmacologic inhibition.
Figure type: pathway diagram for a review article.
Audience/context: immunology or inflammation review.
Canvas: landscape 16:9, clean white background.

Layout:
Use clear cellular compartments: extracellular cytokine at the top, plasma membrane receptor in the upper center, cytoplasmic JAK and STAT activation in the middle, nucleus and gene transcription at the bottom right. Add small inhibitor callouts near JAK and STAT activation.

Main visual elements:
- cytokine ligand
- cytokine receptor dimer
- JAK1/JAK2
- phosphorylated STAT
- STAT dimer
- nuclear translocation
- inflammatory gene transcription
- JAK inhibitor
- STAT pathway blockade

Mechanism flow:
1. Cytokine binds a cell-surface receptor complex.
2. Receptor dimerization activates JAK1/JAK2.
3. JAK proteins phosphorylate STAT transcription factors.
4. STAT proteins dimerize in the cytoplasm.
5. STAT dimers translocate into the nucleus.
6. Nuclear STAT complexes activate inflammatory gene transcription.
7. JAK inhibitors reduce STAT phosphorylation and downstream signaling.

Labels to include exactly:
Cytokine, Receptor, JAK1/JAK2, pSTAT, STAT dimer, Nucleus, Inflammatory genes, JAK inhibitor.

Visual style:
Crisp vector-like pathway illustration, clean compartment boundaries, simple membrane and nucleus shapes, muted blue, teal, coral, gold, and gray palette, activation arrows, inhibition bars, consistent line weight, short legible labels, polished review schematic style.

Avoid:
photorealistic cell rendering, dense biochemical network, copied commercial icons, logos, watermark, fake tiny labels, excessive arrows, dark background, decorative gradients.
```
