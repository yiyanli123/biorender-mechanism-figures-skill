# Antibody-Drug Conjugate Mechanism

## Use Case

Drug mechanism schematic showing antibody-drug conjugate binding, internalization, payload release, and tumor cell killing.

## Layout Plan

- Canvas: landscape 16:9
- Reading direction: left to right
- Zones: extracellular binding, endosomal internalization, cytotoxic payload effect
- Main visual hierarchy: ADC lifecycle

## Ready-to-Use Prompt

```text
Create a publication-ready biomedical schematic, BioRender-inspired but fully original.

Topic: Antibody-drug conjugate mechanism of action in antigen-positive tumor cells.
Figure type: drug mechanism schematic.
Audience/context: translational oncology review or investor science deck.
Canvas: landscape 16:9, clean white background.

Layout:
Four sequential panels in one continuous left-to-right flow: target binding, receptor-mediated internalization, lysosomal linker cleavage, cytotoxic payload activity. Use one large tumor cell membrane spanning the first two panels and an intracellular zoom-in for the final two panels.

Main visual elements:
- antigen-positive tumor cell
- surface tumor antigen
- antibody-drug conjugate
- cleavable linker
- cytotoxic payload
- endosome
- lysosome
- microtubules
- apoptotic tumor cell

Mechanism flow:
1. ADC binds a tumor-associated surface antigen.
2. The ADC-antigen complex is internalized by endocytosis.
3. Lysosomal processing cleaves the linker.
4. Cytotoxic payload is released inside the tumor cell.
5. Payload disrupts microtubules or DNA damage response.
6. Tumor cell undergoes apoptosis.

Labels to include exactly:
ADC, Tumor antigen, Internalization, Lysosome, Payload release, Microtubule disruption, Apoptosis.

Visual style:
Crisp vector-like biomedical mechanism figure, clean panel spacing, antibody Y-shape with small payload dots, smooth tumor cell forms, muted blue, teal, magenta, gold, and gray palette, consistent arrows, subtle shadows, short readable labels, polished therapeutic mechanism style.

Avoid:
photorealistic cells, overly glossy 3D drug render, copied icons, logos, watermark, tiny fake labels, crowded molecular detail, dark background.
```
