# Neuroinflammation Cascade

## Use Case

Review schematic showing how peripheral inflammation activates the blood-brain barrier, microglia, astrocytes, and neuronal stress pathways.

## Layout Plan

- Canvas: landscape 16:9
- Reading direction: top-left peripheral trigger to bottom-right neuronal injury
- Zones: blood vessel, blood-brain barrier, brain parenchyma
- Main visual hierarchy: barrier activation and glial crosstalk

## Ready-to-Use Prompt

```text
Create a publication-ready biomedical schematic, BioRender-inspired but fully original.

Topic: Peripheral inflammation driving neuroinflammation through blood-brain barrier activation, microglial priming, and astrocyte reactivity.
Figure type: review schematic.
Audience/context: neuroscience review article.
Canvas: landscape 16:9, clean white or very light gray background.

Layout:
Two connected compartments. Place a blood vessel and endothelial blood-brain barrier across the upper left. Place brain parenchyma with microglia, astrocytes, and neurons across the lower right. Use a clear diagonal flow from peripheral cytokines to neuronal stress.

Main visual elements:
- blood vessel lumen
- endothelial cell barrier
- circulating IL-6 and TNF-alpha
- activated microglia
- reactive astrocyte
- stressed neuron
- complement C1q/C3 signaling
- synapse loss

Mechanism flow:
1. Peripheral cytokines increase endothelial activation.
2. Blood-brain barrier permeability rises.
3. Microglia become primed and release inflammatory mediators.
4. Astrocytes become reactive and amplify cytokine signaling.
5. Complement activation promotes synapse pruning.
6. Neurons show mitochondrial stress and reduced synaptic connectivity.

Labels to include exactly:
IL-6, TNF-alpha, BBB activation, Microglia, Reactive astrocyte, C1q/C3, Synapse loss, Neuronal stress.

Visual style:
Clean vector-like biomedical illustration, soft glial cell morphology, restrained blue, teal, violet, coral, and neutral gray palette, consistent arrow style, clear compartment boundaries, short labels, subtle shadows, polished review-figure appearance.

Avoid:
photorealistic brain tissue, horror-like inflammation, copied icons, logos, watermarks, dense paragraphs, fake small text, tangled arrows, dark gradient background.
```
