# Gut Microbiome Metabolite Axis

## Use Case

Graphical abstract for a microbiome paper showing how diet-derived metabolites regulate epithelial barrier function and immune tone.

## Layout Plan

- Canvas: square
- Reading direction: circular flow from diet to microbes to metabolites to host response
- Zones: gut lumen, epithelial barrier, lamina propria immune cells
- Main visual hierarchy: metabolite-mediated host signaling

## Ready-to-Use Prompt

```text
Create a publication-ready biomedical schematic, BioRender-inspired but fully original.

Topic: Gut microbiome-derived short-chain fatty acids linking dietary fiber to epithelial barrier integrity and regulatory immune responses.
Figure type: graphical abstract.
Audience/context: microbiome research article.
Canvas: square, clean white background.

Layout:
Use a balanced circular flow. Put dietary fiber and gut microbes in the upper left gut lumen, short-chain fatty acids in the center, epithelial barrier across the middle, and immune regulation in the lower right lamina propria.

Main visual elements:
- dietary fiber particles
- commensal bacteria
- acetate, propionate, butyrate molecules
- intestinal epithelial cells
- tight junction proteins
- goblet cell mucus
- regulatory T cell
- dendritic cell
- anti-inflammatory cytokines

Mechanism flow:
1. Dietary fiber reaches the gut lumen.
2. Commensal bacteria ferment fiber into short-chain fatty acids.
3. Butyrate supports epithelial energy metabolism.
4. Tight junctions and mucus barrier improve.
5. Dendritic cells promote regulatory T cell differentiation.
6. Regulatory T cells release IL-10 to maintain immune tolerance.

Labels to include exactly:
Dietary fiber, Commensal bacteria, SCFAs, Butyrate, Tight junctions, Treg, IL-10.

Visual style:
Original vector-like biomedical graphical abstract, soft rounded cells and bacteria, friendly but scientific palette using teal, green, gold, coral, and neutral gray, subtle shadows, clean arrows, minimal labels, polished journal-cover-adjacent composition.

Avoid:
photorealistic intestine, cartoonish mascots, copied commercial icons, logos, watermark, dense explanatory text, fake tiny labels, dark background, excessive decorative particles.
```
